Arithmetic geometry & algebraic geometry

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the Department of Mathematics at Southern University of Science and Technology (SUSTech), supervised by Prof. Yong Hu. I earned my bachelor’s degree from Southwest Jiaotong University in 2024 and master’s degree from Southern University of Science and Technology in 2026, both with a focus on Mathematics.

My research interests are in arithmetic geometry, algebraic geometry and related topics. Especially, I focus on local approximations of rational and integral points on algebraic varieties and their Brauer–Manin obstructions; Galois cohomology of linear algebraic groups and quadratic forms; period-index problems of central simple algebras. Currently, I am now (probably or soon) working mainly on two projects: adelic points on closed subvarieties of tori over number fields; period-index problems and Kato-Kazumaki conjecture over semi-global fields.

Research interests

  • Local approximation of rational and integral points on varieties. Local-global principles, strong approximation and Brauer–Manin obstructions.
  • Period-index problems of central simple algebras Kato-Kazumaki conjecture and Serre II conjecture
  • Rationality and related topics on birational geometry. Rationality of algebraic varieties, arithmetic of rationally connected varieties.
  • Arithmetic models and curves over CDVRs Models of varieties and applications in arithmetic, patching methods.
  • Galois cohomology of linear algebraic groups and quadratic forms. u-invariant of quadratic forms, Milnor K-theory.
